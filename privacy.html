Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026

This policy covers AutoCraft Publisher, an internal tool that Craft Canvas Builds uses to publish its own videos, and this website.

What AutoCraft Publisher is

AutoCraft Publisher uploads videos made by Craft Canvas Builds to Craft Canvas Builds' own YouTube channel, and schedules them on our own social media accounts. It is operated only by the Craft Canvas Builds team. It has no other users, and it is not offered to the public.

Google and YouTube data we access

AutoCraft Publisher uses YouTube API Services. When we sign it in to our own YouTube channel, it asks Google for:

  • YouTube upload access (youtube.upload): to upload our videos with their titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails.
  • YouTube read-only access (youtube.readonly): to read our own channel's name and ID, so it uploads to the right channel, and to check each uploaded video's privacy status.

It does not read, collect or store data about viewers, subscribers, commenters or any other YouTube users.

How the data is stored

The sign-in token Google issues is stored only on the computer that runs AutoCraft Publisher. Upload results (video IDs and links) are kept in a local log on that same computer. Nothing is stored on any server we run.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone. We do not use it for advertising or analytics, and we do not use it to train AI models.

AutoCraft Publisher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions. When it is revoked, the stored token stops working, and we delete it from the computer that runs the tool. You can also ask us to delete data by emailing the address below.

YouTube and Google policies

By using AutoCraft Publisher with a YouTube account, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or trackers, and it does not collect personal information. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
